Account & Data Deletion Request

Application: Learn Linux: Commands & Admin (com.anintellectualcompany.linuxQuiz) • Developer: An Intellectual Company

How to Delete Your Account and Data

In full compliance with Google Play Developer Policies on User Data, we provide simple ways for users to permanently delete their account, quiz history, XP, streak progress, and bookmarks.

Option 1: Direct In-App Deletion (Instant)

1. Open the Learn Linux: Commands & Admin app on your device.
2. Tap the Profile or Settings icon.
3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account & Data.
4. Confirm the prompt. Your user record in Firebase Auth and all Firestore documents will be erased immediately.

Option 2: Web Deletion Request via Email

If you have already uninstalled the application and wish to delete your cloud data:

Send an email to anintellectualcompany@gmail.com with:

  • Subject: Account Deletion Request - Linux App
  • Registered Google Email: The Gmail address you used with Google Sign-In in the app.

Our team will process the permanent deletion within 48 hours and send you a confirmation email.

What Data is Deleted?

  • Google account identifier & email link in Firebase Auth
  • Cloud Firestore user document containing streak counts, XP, level, and unlocked achievements
  • Saved question bookmarks and custom quiz records
