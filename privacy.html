Privacy Policy

Application: Linux Admin: Learn & Commands • Effective Date: August 31, 2026 • Developer: An Intellectual Company

1. Introduction

Welcome to Linux Admin: Learn & Commands ("we", "our", or "us"), developed and published by An Intellectual Company. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how data is handled.

2. What Information We Collect

A. Learning Progress & Sync (Optional)

You can use the entire application as a guest without creating an account. If you sign in with Google Sign-In, we store your learning streak, quiz XP, and bookmarked commands securely in Google Firebase so your progress syncs across your devices.

B. App Performance & Crash Reports

We use Firebase Crashlytics to monitor application stability. If the app encounters an unexpected error, anonymous technical diagnostics (such as device model and crash logs) are collected strictly to help us fix the issue.

C. Advertisements

To keep the app free, we display non-intrusive advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may process standard advertising identifiers in compliance with Google Play Developer policies.

3. How We Use Device Features

  • Internet Access: Used to sync your quiz scores, fetch new lesson content, and load advertisements.
  • Notifications: Used only if you opt-in to daily study reminders or streak preservation alerts. You can turn this off anytime in your phone's notification settings.
  • Haptic Feedback: Provides subtle vibration feedback when answering quiz questions.

4. Data Control & Account Deletion

You have full control over your data at all times. You can delete your account and all associated cloud data directly inside the app under Settings ➡️ Profile ➡️ Delete Account, or by emailing us at anintellectualcompany.contact@gmail.com.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app is designed for students, developers, and system administrators. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 years of age.
