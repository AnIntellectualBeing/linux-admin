Privacy Policy

Application: Learn Linux: Commands & Admin • Package Name: com.anintellectualcompany.linuxQuiz • Effective Date: August 31, 2026 • Developer: An Intellectual Company

1. Introduction

Welcome to Learn Linux: Commands & Admin ("we", "our", or "us"), developed and published by An Intellectual Company. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, stored, used, and shared when you use the Learn Linux mobile application on Android devices.

2. Information We Collect

A. Optional Account Information

You can use the application fully as a guest without creating an account. If you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In (powered by Firebase Authentication), we collect:

  • Profile Data: Display name, email address, and profile photo URL.
  • Learning Progress & Activity: Quiz scores, daily streak status, saved bookmarks, XP, and unlocked achievements stored securely in Google Cloud Firestore so you can sync across multiple devices.

B. Diagnostic & Performance Data

We use Firebase Crashlytics to identify bugs, crashes, and performance issues. Crashlytics collects anonymous technical diagnostic data including device model, operating system version, and stack traces at the time of a crash.

C. Advertising Identifiers (Google AdMob)

To keep the app free to use, we display ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may process the Google Advertising ID (AD_ID) and device parameters in accordance with Google Play Developer policies.

D. Push Notification Tokens

If you enable daily streak reminders, Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) generates a device token used solely to deliver scheduled study alerts.

3. Device Permissions Used

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required for syncing bookmarks, quiz progress, and loading AdMob ads.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Verifies network availability before attempting cloud sync.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Delivers optional daily challenge alerts and streak reminders (Android 13+).
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Provides haptic feedback during quiz interactions.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

Our application integrates with Google Play Services and Firebase. Review their policies below:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob & Ads Policy

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You have full control over your personal data. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time:

How to request immediate account & data deletion:

1. In the app: Navigate to Settings ➡️ Profile ➡️ Delete Account.
2. On the web: Visit our Account Deletion Portal.
3. By Email: Contact us at anintellectualcompany@gmail.com. We will permanently delete your user record within 48 hours.

6. Children's Privacy

The app is intended for general audiences, students, and IT professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 years of age.

7. Security

All network traffic is encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
